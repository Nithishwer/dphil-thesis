Preparation details

Selected exactly TYR X188, ASN X192, ASN X348, GLU X412 (biological E622),
and unk X915. All 108 selected PDB atoms, including their hydrogens, retain
their original coordinates. No water, lipid, ion, or other residue is included.
Original atoms are ordered first, by the requested residue order and PDB order;
48 synthetic cap atoms follow. atom_map.tsv records all 0-based indices.

Each isolated residue has CH3-C(=O)- (acetyl, ACE) at its N boundary and
-NH-CH3 (N-methylamide, NME) at its C boundary. Caps retain peptide amide
chemistry, have explicit hydrogens, and have been relaxed with MMFF94s while
all original atoms were fixed. Nearby excluded peptide atoms supplied only
orientation vectors; none were extracted into the cluster.

Ligand heavy-atom connectivity and bond orders were mapped from ligand.sdf.
All four ligand stereocenters agree with the SDF. PDB protonation is retained:
N3x is NH3+, the ligand carboxyl group is COO-, and GLU412 is COO-. The SDF
is a neutral NH2/COOH reference, so its proton locations were not substituted
for the PDB's zwitterionic ligand. The structural formal-charge sum is -1;
the requested ORCA charge 0 and multiplicity 2 define a 625-electron radical
state, one electron fewer than the conventional closed-shell formal-charge
assignment. No proton has been added or removed to change that choice.

ORCA freezes every heavy atom (including the caps) with Cartesian constraints.
All hydrogens may relax, subject to the H2x--OE1 distance scan. The exact
starting distance is calculated from the unchanged PDB coordinates.
ORCA is not installed here; the input was checked structurally and against
the documented ORCA scan/constraint syntax, but no QM calculation was run.
